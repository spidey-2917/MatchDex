## 🌟 Legacy Release Highlights
We've just pushed a massive update to MatchDex! Here is a breakdown of all the exciting new features and changes you will see:

## 🌟 New Rarity System & Premium Cards
We have completely overhauled the rarity system. The old rarities have been replaced with a new Tier-based system (ranging from Common I-III up to Ultimate I-III) to make card progression more meaningful.

**Premium Cards**: Premium cards are a special tier of cards that offer unique benefits and are extremely rare. They can be of different subtypes like Custom, SBC, or Paid. These cards are designed for true collectors and are highly sought after!

## ⚡ Card Stat Boosts
Cards can now receive individual stat boosts! A card might have an inherent bonus to its attack or defence, representing a special edition or in-form status.
- You can now see these boosts visually when viewing a card. For example, you might see `ATK: 95 (+5%)` in the `/md show` and card dropdown menus.
- These percentage boosts automatically apply during match simulations, making these cards stronger on the pitch!

## 👔 Manager System
You can now acquire and equip **Managers** to lead your team! 
- Each Manager has a **Preferred Formation** and a specific **Boost Type** (Attacking, Defence, or Counter Attacking).
- **Synergy Bonus**: If your equipped manager's preferred formation matches your active lineup's formation, your entire team receives a massive bonus! 
  - *Attacking* managers give your team a +5% global attack boost.
  - *Defence* managers give your team a +5% global defence boost.
  - *Counter Attacking* managers increase your team's chance to launch deadly counter-attacks by +10%!

## ⚽ Simulation Engine Refinements
We've tweaked the match simulation engine to ensure that your team's stats matter more than ever. The simulation now relies **85% on your team's raw stats and tactics**, with only a 15% luck variance. Build the best team, equip the right manager, and dominate the competition!
