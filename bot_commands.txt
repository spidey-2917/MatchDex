# MatchDex Bot Command List

## General Commands
- /stats: Show your basic player stats (points, wins, losses, etc.).
- /collection [sort_by]: View your card collection with optional sorting.
- /show <player_name>: View detailed information and the card image for a specific player.
- /last: View details of the most recent card you caught.
- /leaderboard [scope]: View the points leaderboard (Server or Global).

## Information & Utility
- /about: Get information about the MatchDex bot, its stats, and links.
- /help: View a categorized list of all available commands.
- /favourite <card_id>: Toggle a card as a favorite (marked with a ❤️ in collection).
- /completion: View your collection completion progress by card type.

## Team & Squad Management
- /team_begin: Start building a squad by selecting a formation (resets current lineup).
- /team_view [member]: View your own or another member's active squad and power.
- /team_add <slot> <player_name>: Assign a card from your collection to a specific formation slot.
- /team_auto: Automatically fill empty squad slots with your highest-rated available cards.
- /team_remove <slot>: Remove a player from a specific slot in your squad.
- /team_sub <slot> <player_name>: Assign a player to your bench (substitute slots).

## Matches & Wagers
- /match_start <opponent>: Challenge another user to a standard MatchDex game.
- /match_cancel: Cancel your current active match.
- /wager challenge <user>: Challenge a user to a high-stakes Penalty Shootout wager.
- /wager add <card_id>: Add a card from your collection to your wager stakes.

## Packs & Promotions
- /pack_daily: Open your daily pack (Base cards, 24h cooldown).
- /pack_weekly: Open your weekly icon pack (Icon cards, 7d cooldown).
- /pack_event: Open your weekly event pack (Event cards, 7d cooldown).
- /pack_premium: Open a premium pack (Icon/Event cards, 48h cooldown, requires Premium).
- /promo <code>: Redeem a promo code for points or card rewards.

## Trading
- /trade start <user>: Initiate a trade request with another player.
- /trade add <card_id>: Add a card to your current trade offer.
- /trade remove <card_id>: Remove a card from your current trade offer.
- /trade bulk_add [sort_by]: Add multiple cards to a trade via a selection menu.

## User Profile & Data Management
- /md list [sort_by]: View your entire card collection with sorting options.
- /md show <card_id/player>: View detailed information and the card image for a specific card you own or a player.
- /md give <user> <card_id>: Gift a player card from your collection to another user.
- /md export: Download a JSON file containing all your user data.
- /md delete: Permanently delete your MatchDex profile and all your data.

## Server Configuration (Administrator Permission Required)
- /config spawn_channel <channel>: Set the channel where cards will automatically spawn.
- /config log_channel <channel>: Set the channel for server-specific administration logs.

## Bot Administration (Bot Admin Only)
### Spawn Tools
- /admin spawn random [count]: Manually spawn catchable cards in the current channel.
- /admin spawn card <player_name>: Manually spawn a specific player card.

### Economy & Cards
- /admin give <user> <player_search>: Directly give a specific card to a user.
- /admin give_full <user> [include_all]: Give a user every card in the database.
- /admin md count [user]: View card counts for a user or global database totals.

### Management & Blacklist
- /admin md access <user> <action>: Grant or revoke Bot Admin permissions.
- /admin md reload_blacklist: Refresh the blacklist cache from the database.
- /admin md sync: Manually sync the global slash command tree with Discord.
- /admin blacklist add <user> [reason]: Blacklist a user from using the bot.
- /admin blacklist-guild add <guild_id> [reason]: Blacklist an entire server from using the bot.

### Logging & History
- /admin logs catchlogs [channel]: Configure the global catch logging channel.
- /admin logs commandlogs [channel]: Configure the global command logging channel.
- /admin info user <user>: View deep database information for a specific user.
- /admin info guild [guild_id]: View technical details for a specific server.
- /admin history user <user>: View a user's recent catch and pack opening history.
- /admin history card <card_id>: Track the history and ownership of a specific card.
- /admin status [status] [text]: Change the bot's status (Online/Idle/DND) and activity text.
- /admin rarity: View the distribution of card rarities across the entire database.
- /admin cooldown [guild_id]: Check the message spawn cooldown status for a server.
