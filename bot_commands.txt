MatchDex Bot Commands
=====================

/about - Get information about this bot
/collection - Show your card collection summary
/completion - Show how much of the card collection you
/favourite - No description
/help - Overview of all available commands
/last - Show your most recently caught card
/leaderboard - Show the leaderboard
/match_cancel - Cancel your active match
/match_start - Challenge another user to a Matchdex game
/pack_daily - Pack a daily base player card
/pack_event - Pack a weekly event card
/pack_open - Open a custom or stashed pack by name
/pack_premium - No description
/pack_wallet - View your stashed packs and open them
/pack_weekly - Pack a weekly icon card
/promo - Enter a promo code for a reward
/sbc - Complete a Squad Building Challenge
/shop - Get a link to the MatchDex store
/stats - Show your basic stats
/team_add - Add a player from your collection to a squad slot
/team_auto - Auto-fill your squad with the best cards from your collection
/team_begin - Create a new squad by selecting a formation
/team_remove - Remove a player from a slot
/team_sub - Set a substitute player on the bench
/team_view - View a squad with details
